# Privacy Policy

**YouTube Channel Search Sorter** by Iterix

**Effective Date:** April 14, 2026

## Data Collection

This extension does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or user information.

## Local Storage

The extension uses the browser's local storage API (`chrome.storage.local`) solely to save your sort and filter preferences. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to Iterix or any third party.

## Host Permissions

The extension requires access to `youtube.com` to inject the sort toolbar into YouTube channel search pages. It does not read, collect, or transmit any information from YouTube or any other website.

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not communicate with any external servers, APIs, or third-party services.

## Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this privacy policy will be reflected in this document and in the extension's repository at https://github.com/Iterix-US/YT-Channel-SearchSorter.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at https://github.com/Iterix-US/YT-Channel-SearchSorter/issues.
